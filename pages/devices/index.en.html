Important: Bluetooth pairing alone does not guarantee compatibility—the remote must implement a supported ATVV/audio and HID protocol. Likewise, changing your input tool to Toggle (click-to-start/stop) will not keep a push-to-talk remote streaming audio once you release its physical button. Third-party compatibles (such as “G9N9N-compatible” clones) require per-batch verification.

Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2 Pro

Model: RC003 · Direct Bluetooth
Supported
  • Hardware: Slim single-sided body with a circular D-pad and bottom USB-C charging port.
  • Voice behavior: Hold the voice button to speak and release to finish (~60-second single-hold hardware cap). Wait briefly after release for tail audio and text submission.
  • Button mapping: Remappable single, double, and long presses. Defaults: Home switches apps, TV plays/pauses media, Menu opens context menu, Power sends Backspace, Back sends Escape.

T1 Air-Mouse & Keyboard Remote

Model: T1-Remote · Direct Bluetooth (not 2.4 GHz dongle)
Sample verified
  • Hardware: Dual-sided remote with front D-pad, 7 remappable front control buttons (Back, Home, Menu, Volume+, Volume−, Mute, Power), plus a rear QWERTY keyboard and air mouse that pass through natively.
  • Hands-free toggle: On the tested Bluetooth sample, press and release once to start recording, then press again to stop. Tested up to nearly 2 minutes continuously (keepalive every 10s, protective cutoff around 5 minutes).
  • Compatibility note: Lookalike clones can run different firmware—verify Bluetooth voice capability and return policy before buying.

Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2

Model: RC001 · Direct Bluetooth
Audio pending
  • Model distinction: RC001 is Xiaomi Remote 2, distinct from RC003 (2 Pro). Do not assume 2 Pro audio acceptance automatically covers RC001.
  • Current status: Protocol and button adapters are in place; end-to-end audio validation on RC001 hardware is still pending.

Google Chromecast Voice Remote

Model: G9N9N · 2× AAA batteries
Partial (Voice + Combo)
  • Voice behavior: Requires holding the Google Assistant button for the entire utterance; audio stops immediately upon release (always use Hold mode).
  • Button restriction: On the tested sample, non-compliant key/release report lengths prevent macOS from forwarding ordinary single-button presses. When mapping and seizure are active, short-pressing OK + any direction sends Enter.
  • Compatible clones: Community reports (issue #170) note low-cost compatible units with recognized keys but faster battery drain; each clone batch varies.

Planned
